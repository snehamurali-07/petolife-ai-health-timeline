### Product Name

**PetOlife AI Health Timeline**

### One-liner

> **Turn scattered pet health records into one intelligent, personalized health story.**
> 

### Problem

Pet parents often have health information scattered across:

- Veterinary invoices
- Blood/lab reports
- Vaccination certificates
- Prescriptions
- Medical PDFs
- Photos of documents
- Manually entered information

The problem isn't simply **storing** these records.

The real problem is **understanding them over time**.

A pet parent may know:

> "Luna had a blood test in March."
> 

But they may not immediately understand:

> "Her weight has changed over the last few months, this lab value has appeared repeatedly, and her vaccination is approaching renewal."
> 

### Solution

PetOlife AI Health Timeline converts raw pet-health documents and records into:

**Raw Data → Structured Events → Health Timeline → AI Insights → Responsible Actions**

The system uses AI to extract relevant information from PDFs/images and organize it chronologically.

The timeline then helps answer:

> **What happened?**
> 

> **What changed?**
> 

> **What patterns exist?**
> 

> **What may need attention?**
>
